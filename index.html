01 / About

Research with human learning at its center.

I am a Computer Science Ph.D. student at Georgia State University, advised by Dr. Anu G. Bourgeois. My work explores AI in education, simulated learners, interaction-centered assessment, and preference-based model alignment. I am interested in building AI systems that better reflect how people learn, reason, and make decisions.

02 / Selected Research

Projects & research directions

Research spanning simulated learning, transparent assessment, and alignment of AI models.

PROJECT 01

Simulated Students with Learning Mechanisms

Investigating whether LLM-based simulated students can model learning processes and provide reliable evaluation environments for AI tutors. Comparing proficiency, knowledge-state, and behavioral variants using explicit knowledge updates, pre/post assessments, and multi-turn tutoring interactions.

Ongoing Research
PROJECT 02

Interaction-Centered Assignment & Assessment

Developed a two-agent framework that makes student reasoning visible and generates evidence-grounded assessment summaries. Conducted a two-phase study involving 12 undergraduate students and 15 teaching assistants; built a prototype with React, Node.js/Express, and Ollama.

Human–AI Learning
PROJECT 03

Preference Alignment for Multimodal Models

Studied language-agent preference feedback for tuning vision–language models using direct preference optimization (DPO), evaluated on multimodal understanding benchmarks.
